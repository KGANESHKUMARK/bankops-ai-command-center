# Incident Communication and Escalation Policy

> SYNTHETIC DEMO DATA - fictional policy for a fictional bank.

## Severity Levels

- Critical: customer-facing payments or regulatory deadlines at immediate risk. Update every 30 minutes.
- High: significant degradation with a workaround. Update every hour.
- Medium: limited impact, no deadline risk. Update every four hours.
- Low: informational or monitoring items. Daily update.

## Escalation

1. The incident commander owns communication and decisions.
2. Critical incidents page the duty operations manager immediately.
3. Regulatory deadline risk is escalated to the regulatory reporting lead.
4. Vendor-caused incidents follow the vendor escalation contacts held by the service owner.

## Human Accountability

AI-assisted investigations provide recommendations only. Humans approve changes according to the change management
standard, and every action is recorded in the audit log.
